## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end architectural luxury and austere editorial restraint. Rooted in the rigorous typography of Swiss modernism and tempered by the warmth of archival exhibition catalogues, it merges stark geometric precision with an intimate, tactile warmth.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Restraint:** Zero decorative gradients, drop shadows, or skeuomorphic blurs. Visual weight is achieved solely through scale contrast, negative space, and disciplined hairline framing.
- **Warm Architectural Atmosphere:** Crisp, gallery-grade structural rhythm softened by an ivory-and-gold foundation. The presence of warm charcoal and muted champagne gold conveys permanence, discretion, and connoisseurship.
- **Severe Planarity:** Flat planes, sharp geometric boundaries, and unyielding baseline alignment emulate finely bound architectural monographs and high-fashion broadsheets.

## Colors

The color system transitions from sterile cold monochrome to an archival ivory and champagne gold hierarchy while maintaining uncompromising contrast and legibility.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Ink (`#181613`):** Deep warm charcoal. Used for primary headlines, structural anchors, solid button fills, and high-impact text. Avoids harsh synthetic black in favor of carbon print ink.
- **Accent Gold (`#C5A059` / `#D4AF37`):** Muted champagne gold. Used sparingly for interactive accents, active states, key numerals, highlighting rules, and focused states. Must never be used as a gradient.
- **Subtle Gold Border (`#E8DEC8`):** Warm structural hairline tone. Defines grid divisions, container boundaries, table rules, and framing outlines without visual noise.
- **Ivory Canvas (`#FBF8F1`):** Warm, unbleached cotton paper tone. Serves as the primary viewport and document canvas.
- **Elevated Surface (`#F4EFE6`):** Tonal ivory container fill for card backgrounds, input beds, and hover fills.
- **Supporting Ink (`#544F46` & `#766F64`):** Mid-tone warm grays for secondary narratives, metadata timestamps, and subdued interface labels.

## Typography

Typography pairs the technical, idiosyncratic proportions of Space Grotesk with the refined utilitarian rhythm of Hanken Grotesk.

- **Headlines & Display:** Space Grotesk delivers deliberate geometric character and sharp angles. Tight tracking across display scales creates unified, solid typographic shapes that echo architectural facades.
- **Body & Longform:** Hanken Grotesk offers open counters and precise geometry, sustaining clear legibility across narrative descriptions, project summaries, and specifications.
- **Labels & Micro-Indices:** Set in uppercase Space Grotesk with expanded letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to act as technical annotations and catalogue indexing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by an asymmetric column structure that treats empty space as a structural material, prioritizing pacing over density.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Outer margin: `1.25rem`, gutters: `1.5rem`. Modules stack vertically; media runs flush to margins or full-bleed.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid. Margins expand to `2.5rem` with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at `1680px`. Margins widen to `4rem` with `2.5rem` gutters to permit wide gutters, offset side notes, and asymmetric project layouts.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical rhythm uses macro tokens (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between sections to create intentional white space reminiscent of luxury print layouts.
- Component-level spacing uses `space-xs` to `space-md` to maintain structured internal alignment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed purely through planar surface shifts and hairline framing. Shadows, blur filters, and gradients are excluded.

- **Planar Tiering:** Depth is established by placing elevated surfaces (`#F4EFE6`) against the canvas (`#FBF8F1`), creating soft tonal contrast without elevation shadows.
- **Hairline Framing:** Section rules, table dividers, and containment perimeters use 1px solid borders in subtle gold (`#E8DEC8`).
- **Modal & Inversion Layers:** Overlays, navigation curtains, and focal state modules invert the background completely to `#181613` with warm ivory (`#FBF8F1`) and gold (`#C5A059`) text, creating impact through stark contrast rather than dropped shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`).

- All containers, inputs, buttons, media frames, and contextual overlays feature crisp 90-degree right angles (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Rectilinear lines honor physical print sheets, architectural drafts, and trimmed mounts. Rounded geometries are permitted only for literal circular status bullets or navigational indicators embedded as typographic glyphs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid warm charcoal (`#181613`) fill with warm ivory (`#FBF8F1`) typography (`label-md`). 0px radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Border: 1px solid `#181613`. Hover state: Fills with `#C5A059` with `#181613` text.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#E8DEC8` border and `#181613` text. Hover state: Border shifts to `#C5A059` with a subtle `#F4EFE6` surface fill.
- **Tertiary Action:** Borderless `label-md` in `#181613` paired with an accent gold arrow (`→` in `#C5A059`). Hover state: 1px bottom border in `#C5A059` with a 4px offset.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp rectangular badges with `#F4EFE6` fill and a 1px solid `#E8DEC8` border.
- Text: Space Grotesk `label-sm` in `#544F46` or `#C5A059` for highlighted status. Padding: `0.25rem 0.625rem`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Flat `#F4EFE6` surface with a 1px solid `#E8DEC8` border. Padding: `0.875rem 1rem`. Typography: `body-md` in `#181613`. Focus state: Solid 1px `#C5A059` border with no box-shadow glow. Placeholder: `#766F64`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16x16px sharp squares. Default state: 1px `#E8DEC8` border on `#FBF8F1`. Selected state: `#181613` solid fill with a centered `#C5A059` square mark.

### Cards & Presentation Frames
- Built flat without shadows. Modules sit directly on the canvas bounded by optional 1px hairline rules in `#E8DEC8` or a `#F4EFE6` surface fill.
- Captions, index labels, and title groups sit outside or directly flush against image frames, using uppercase `label-sm` metadata anchors in `#C5A059`.

### Lists & Index Rows
- Horizontal index rows separated by 1px solid `#E8DEC8` rules.
- Asymmetrical columns displaying index numbers (`01`, `02`) in `#C5A059`, title in `headline-sm` (`#181613`), category tags in `#544F46`, and date aligned to the right. Hover state: Smooth background shift to `#F4EFE6`.

### Exhibition Header & Top Navigation
- Anchored with a bottom 1px hairline rule in `#E8DEC8`. Background: `#FBF8F1`.
- Navigation items use uppercase `label-md` with active states marked by a 1px underline in `#C5A059`.
